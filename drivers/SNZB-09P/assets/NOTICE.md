The icon.svg and learnmode.svg outlines is adapted from drivers/HS2WD-E/assets/icon.svg in
com.zigbee.heiman (https://github.com/jeffersvdwiel/com.zigbee.heiman).
The original HEIMAN lettering was removed and replaced with SONOFF vector lettering.
The PNG images are renders of the modified SVG, rather than photographs of the HEIMAN device.
